About Quantum Trading AI

Explore real market charts and practice strategies in a simulated trading workspace.

Learn with a clearer view of the market

Quantum Trading AI combines TradingView chart embeds with paper-trading tools for stocks, crypto, forex, and commodities. Use the charts to explore market price action, then test ideas in a virtual portfolio without placing real orders.

What you can do

  • TradingView charts: Open instrument-specific charts from the dashboard, market board, simulator, and share analysis pages. Chart intervals and available symbols vary by instrument.
  • Paper trading: Practice long and short orders with a virtual wallet and review positions in your trade journal.
  • Market exploration: Browse watchlists and sample market summaries, then open a TradingView chart for a closer look.
  • Educational analysis: Review illustrative signals and trade setups as prompts for learning, not as investment advice.

Understanding the data

TradingView supplies the embedded chart data. Real-time availability and any exchange delay depend on the instrument and TradingView’s data agreements. The prices, changes, AI signals, and paper-order calculations elsewhere in this app are simulated and do not synchronize with chart quotes.

This platform is for educational purposes only. It does not connect to a brokerage, place real orders, or provide financial advice.
