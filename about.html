What is this Platform?

Quantum Trading AI is a sophisticated paper trading simulator designed for both aspiring and experienced traders. It provides a risk-free environment to practice trading strategies across various asset classes like stocks, crypto, and forex. Our platform uses simulated real-time price data and an AI-powered advisory engine to generate technical analysis and trade signals.

Key Features

  • AI Trading Consultant: Get AI-generated signals, technical rationale, and optimal time-lap analysis for various assets.
  • Live Paper Trading: Use a $100,000 virtual wallet to execute simulated buy (long) and sell (short) orders with leverage.
  • Advanced Charts: Analyze price action with integrated TradingView charts, complete with tools and indicators.
  • Comprehensive Journal: Track your active positions and review your trade history to learn from profits and losses.

This tool is for educational and entertainment purposes only. It is designed to help you learn the mechanics of trading without any financial risk.
